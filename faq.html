FAQ

Everything you need to know about Lumina Match.

General

What is Lumina Match?

A curated dating club for health-minded singles in Bangkok. We host in-person events and provide concierge support to help you meet the right people.

Who is Lumina for?

Singles 18+ in Bangkok who value health, intentionality, and real conversation. People who’ve outgrown the bar scene but aren’t ready to settle for swiping.

What does ‘health-minded’ mean?

It means you care about your wellbeing — sleep, movement, being intentional about alcohol. It’s NOT a body type or appearance standard.

Events

What happens at a Lumina event?

Depending on the format: guided conversations, curated seating, structured rotations, and a private matching process at the end. No awkward mingling. No pressure.

What if I don’t match with anyone?

That’s okay! Not every event leads to a match. We’ll invite you to future events that might be a better fit.

What’s the mutual match process?

At the end of each event, you privately select who you’d like to connect with. We only share contact details when both people say yes.

Safety

How do you keep events safe?

Every event has a host and co-host. We have a public code of conduct and zero tolerance for harassment. Private reporting is available throughout.

Is my data private?

Yes. We never share your information with third parties. Contact details are only shared with mutual matches.

Pricing

How much does it cost?

Event tickets typically range from ฿800-1,500 depending on the format. Concierge services are available for members who want more personalized support.
